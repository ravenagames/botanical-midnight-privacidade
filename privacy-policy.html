Privacy Policy — Botanical Midnight!

Data controller: Ravena Games (independent developer) — contact and data protection officer: vnpaese@gmail.com

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018). The rights described in section 11 are available to every player, wherever they live.

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress (scores, records, seeds, achievements, unlocked biomes, saved matches and settings) is stored on your device. The game does not ask for a sign-up, email, phone number, location or access to your contacts.

2. Weekly online ranking (Google Firebase)

To take part in the weekly ranking, the game creates an anonymous identifier (Firebase Authentication, anonymous sign-in) and sends the following to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): that identifier, the nickname you choose (or your Google Play Games name, if you are connected), the match score, the time of submission (recorded by the server), the mode and biome played, the number of shinies and level-11 plants in the match, the highest level reached and the game version. Your nickname and score are visible to other players in the ranking; the anonymous identifier is also readable by anyone using the app. This data is used only to build the ranking, work out the weekly prizes and check for cheating. The game also sends anonymous usage counters (for example: how many matches you played in a day, how long a session lasted, how many rewarded videos were offered and completed, whether you opened the shop or completed a purchase, the game language, and whether you tapped Rate or Share) linked to the same anonymous identifier, only to understand whether the game is being used well and to improve it. These counters contain no name, email, precise location or device data other than the game version. We also record the day's seed balance and the date the game was first opened, to measure the pace of the in-game economy and player retention.

3. Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game may use your display name in the ranking, record achievements and leaderboards, and save a copy of your progress to the Google Play Games cloud, linked to your account, so you can continue on another device. Ravena Games does not receive this data on its own servers.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows Google AdMob ads (a banner outside matches, an interstitial when a match ends and optional rewarded videos, such as the Botanical Pause invitation during play, which only shows the video if you tap to watch). AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, under Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . In countries that require consent (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad, and you can review your choice in Settings › Ad privacy. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in Android settings. Players who buy "Remove ads" no longer see banner or interstitial ads.

5. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Ravena Games does not receive or store payment data.

6. Why we use the data and on what legal basis

  • Ranking, prizes and season achievements: performing the service you asked for by joining the ranking (LGPD, art. 7, V) and our legitimate interest in keeping the competition fair (art. 7, IX).
  • Cheat prevention in the ranking: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).
  • Ads: advertising data is processed by Google AdMob, based on consent and your device's ad settings (art. 7, I).
  • Google Play Games and purchases: performing the service you requested (art. 7, V), under Google's terms.

Ravena Games does not sell personal data and does not build commercial profiles of players.

7. Who the data is shared with

Only with Google (Firebase, Google Play Games, Google Play and AdMob), which acts as a processor or an independent controller depending on the service. There is no other sharing.

8. International transfers

Google's services process data on servers outside Brazil, including in the United States. Google applies contractual safeguards to these transfers. Ranking data is kept in Cloud Firestore in the South America (São Paulo) region whenever the service allows.

9. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete the data or uninstall the game.
  • In the online ranking: your nickname and score stay in the weekly ranking until you ask for deletion (see section 11). There is no automatic deletion for inactivity: erasure is always on request, inside the game or by email. Usage counters stay linked to the anonymous identifier until you delete your data. The anonymous identifier contains no personal data.
  • In Google Play Games and AdMob: according to Google's policies and your Google account settings.

10. Security

Data sent to Firebase, Google Play Games and AdMob travels encrypted (HTTPS). Write access to the ranking requires the player's anonymous identifier and goes through validation rules on the server.

11. Your rights (LGPD, art. 18)

You can ask at any time for: confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about who we share it with, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights:

  • In the game: under Settings › Delete all my data, the game erases local progress, your records in the online ranking, the usage counters linked to your identifier and your anonymous account.
  • By email: vnpaese@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. Include the nickname used in the ranking so we can find your records.
  • Progress saved in Google Play Games can be deleted in Google Play Games' own settings.

You may also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority where you live.

12. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent data, write to vnpaese@gmail.com and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the update date at the top changes and, if the change is significant, the game announces it in the What's New screen.

How to delete your data (Botanical Midnight / Ravena Games)

In the game (fastest):

  1. Open Botanical Midnight! by Ravena Games.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. The game erases the progress on the device, your records in the online ranking, the anonymous usage counters, the game's anonymous account on the server, and replaces the Google Play Games cloud save with an empty one.

Without access to the game: write to vnpaese@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Botanical Midnight” and include the nickname used in the ranking. We reply within 15 days.

What is deleted: ranking nickname and scores, usage counters, the anonymous account identifier, and the game's progress and cloud save.

What is not up to us: purchase history stays with Google Play, and your Google account and Google Play Games data stay with Google (manage them at myaccount.google.com). Uninstalling the game removes only the data on the device.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy: vnpaese@gmail.com.
